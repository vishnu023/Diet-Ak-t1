Privacy Architecture

Physiological Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Core Biometric Protection Mandate

At Dietcraft, we believe your physiological data belongs entirely to you. All continuous wearable data streams (glucose coherence, heart rate variability, blood oxygen level profiles, metabolic age metrics) are stored using bank-grade end-to-end encryption. Keys to decrypt your physiological logs are managed in isolated credential stores.

2. Absolute Data Non-Disclosure

Under no circumstances is your physical identity, blood markers, clinical logs, or metabolic history rented, leased, or sold to insurance brokers, pharmaceutical conglomerates, or third-party marketing companies. All clinical insights produced remain bounded within the Dietcraft.life ecosystem.

3. Indian HIPAA Compliance & DPDP Alignment

Our operations strictly adhere to local Indian healthcare privacy architectures and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act frameworks. We store consent parameters transparently and execute background synchronization only through authenticated developer portals.

4. Wearable Biosensors Synchronization

Syncing with Apple Health, Google Fit, Garmin, Fitbit, or Samsung health APIs is entirely voluntary. This data allows the AI Ahara system to monitor your circadian alignment and bio-energy indices (Prana, Agni, Ojas) to generate real-time dietary suggestions.

5. Retention, Rectification and Erasure

You maintain a perpetual right to request complete data deletion at any point. Once you submit a deletion request via our support panels, all biographical details, clinical history, and telemetry history will be scrubbed from our production servers and backups within 7 standard business days.
